## Our Engineering Philosophy

We approach engineering as a business discipline, not simply a coding exercise.

Our principles:

- **Business-first engineering:** Every system must address a defined operational or commercial problem.
- **Architecture before complexity:** Build a coherent foundation before introducing unnecessary complexity.
- **Scalability by design:** Make deliberate architectural decisions that support future growth.
- **Security and accountability:** Protect data, enforce appropriate access controls, and maintain traceable operations.
- **Automation over repetition:** Reduce manual work through reliable, well-integrated systems.
- **Measurable outcomes:** Evaluate systems through performance, reliability, adoption, and business impact.
- **Continuous improvement:** Build incrementally, test assumptions, measure results, and improve continuously.

## Our Engineering Standards

We value:

- Clean, maintainable and documented code.
- Modular and well-defined system architectures.
- Version control and disciplined collaboration.
- Automated testing and quality assurance.
- Secure development and responsible data handling.
- Reproducible deployments and infrastructure management.
- Clear technical documentation and ownership.
